Reading the results

Green "Tag detected, has NDEF data" — the card uses the NDEF format. You'll see a serial number and any stored records.

Orange "Tag sensed but not readable as NDEF" — very likely what you'll get from an EZ-Link card. Your phone's NFC antenna did detect the card, but EZ-Link uses ISO 14443 Type B / CEPAS, a proprietary protocol, not NDEF. This is a browser/format limitation, not proof the antenna is faulty.

Nothing happens at all when tapping — try tapping a known plain NFC tag (e.g. a blank NTAG213/215 sticker, common and cheap) as a control test. If that also produces nothing, the issue is likely antenna positioning (try the upper-back of the phone, different exact spot) or that NFC is switched off in Android Settings. If a plain tag works but the EZ-Link doesn't, that confirms the format limitation above rather than a hardware problem.
